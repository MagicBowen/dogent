# Why Service-Level Objectives Fail Without Shared Ownership

## Introduction
Many teams define service-level objectives, but few connect them to day-to-day engineering decisions. Without shared ownership, the SLO becomes a reporting artifact instead of an operational tool.

## Shared Ownership in Practice
- Product teams need to understand the user impact of latency and availability.
- Platform teams need to expose dashboards and alert thresholds that are easy to interpret.
- Leadership needs escalation paths tied to business risk, not only technical severity.
