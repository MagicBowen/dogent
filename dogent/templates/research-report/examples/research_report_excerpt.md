# Market Entry Research Summary

## Findings
- Demand is growing in the target segment, but margin pressure is increasing due to new entrants.
- The strongest evidence comes from regulator filings, annual reports, and industry transaction data.

## Analysis
The opportunity is real, but the timing advantage depends on whether the company can differentiate through distribution rather than price.

## Recommendation
- Run a limited pilot in the strongest region first.
- Reassess pricing power after one quarter of acquisition and retention data.
