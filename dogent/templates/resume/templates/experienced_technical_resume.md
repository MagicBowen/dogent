# Experienced Technical Resume Structure

## Header
- Full name
- Target title
- Location
- Email
- Phone
- LinkedIn
- GitHub or portfolio

## Summary
- 2-4 lines covering years of experience, specialization, scale, and standout impact.

## Skills
- 3-6 grouped categories such as languages, backend, cloud, data, observability, security, and leadership.

## Experience
- Title - Company, Location | Dates
  - Impact bullet with measurable outcome
  - Technical execution bullet
  - Leadership, ownership, or process bullet
  - Optional concise tech stack line

## Projects (Optional)
- Only include highly relevant projects with concrete outcomes.

## Education
- Degree - School | Dates

## Certifications (Optional)
- Certification - Issuer | Year
